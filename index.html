01 — Computer Vision

SSA-FITS Space Object Detection & Segmentation

  • Built an end-to-end pipeline for blob and streak detection and segmentation in satellite imagery for Space Situational Awareness, covering 10 high-resolution FITS images (9568×6380 px).
  • Preprocessed FITS data with percentile stretch, 3×3 median filtering and 8-bit quantization, then generated about 700 tiles (1024×1024 px) with zero-padding for boundary handling.
  • Produced YOLO-format segmentation annotations using shape criteria (aspect ratio, eccentricity, area) and rebuilt full-size masks from the tiles.
